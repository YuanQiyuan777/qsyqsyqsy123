About This Project

This project explores how bubble tea has become an important part of student life in Singapore. Many students enjoy bubble tea after class as a way to relax, socialise, and take a break from studying. Through interactive videos, users can explore different bubble tea choices and learn about student lifestyle and drink culture.

Milk Tea Experience

Classic Milk Tea

Milk tea is one of the most popular bubble tea choices among students. It is sweet, creamy, and comforting after a long day of classes.


Fruit Tea Experience

Fresh Fruit Tea

Fruit tea is refreshing and colourful. Many students enjoy it because it feels lighter and is perfect for Singapore's warm weather.


Student Lifestyle

Bubble tea is more than just a drink. It has become part of student culture in Singapore. Students often buy bubble tea after class, during study sessions, or when spending time with friends. Through these daily experiences, bubble tea connects food culture, social interaction, and student life.
